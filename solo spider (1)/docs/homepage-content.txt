10. FAQ SECTION

Section label:





Common questions





Headline:





Everything You Want to Know
Before You Sign Up












Q1: Do I need any technical skills to use Solo Spider?





Not at all. Solo Spider is designed to be used without any coding or technical knowledge. If you can use Gmail, you can use Solo Spider. Setup takes under 5 minutes.












Q2: Will the content actually sound like me?





Yes. Solo Spider learns your brand voice from your existing content, website, and the preferences you set during onboarding. You can always edit and refine drafts before publishing — but most users find they barely need to.












Q3: What platforms does Solo Spider post to?





Instagram, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Facebook, and more. Blog publishing works with WordPress, Webflow, Wix, and custom CMS setups via API.












Q4: What's AEO and GRO — do I really need it?





AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) and GRO (Generative Result Optimisation) help you appear in AI-generated search results — like Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Gemini answers. AI search is growing rapidly. Brands that optimise for it now will have a significant advantage. Yes, you need it.












Q5: How is Solo Spider different from just using ChatGPT?





ChatGPT can write — but it can't publish, schedule, audit, post, or track your SEO and AI visibility. Solo Spider wraps powerful AI writing with an end-to-end marketing workflow. It's the difference between having an ingredient and having a meal.












Q6: Can I manage multiple clients or brands?





Yes. The Agency plan supports 25+ separate client workspaces, each with their own brand settings, content calendars, and reports. You can manage everything without ever logging out.












Q7: Is there a free trial?





Yes — every paid plan comes with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You can also use the Starter plan for free, forever.
